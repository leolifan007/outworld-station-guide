## Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

### Who We Are

Outworld Station Guide is a fan-made community guide site operated by individual contributors.

### Information We Collect

This website may collect information through the following:

**Google AdSense**
We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve interest-based ads. You can manage your ad preferences at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

**Google Analytics (if enabled)**
We may use Google Analytics to analyze visitor data including page views, traffic sources, and device types. This data is anonymous.

### Cookie Usage

This website uses the following types of cookies:

| Cookie Type | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| Essential | Website basic functionality | Session |
| Advertising | Display relevant ads | Varies by advertiser |
| Analytics | Track visitor statistics | Up to 2 years |

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. For more information, visit [AboutCookies.org](https://www.aboutcookies.org/).

### Third-Party Services

This website integrates:
- **Google AdSense** — Ad display
- **Google Translate** — Language translation (via external link)

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their privacy practices.

### Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no internet data transmission can be guaranteed 100% secure.

### Children's Privacy

This website is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting.
