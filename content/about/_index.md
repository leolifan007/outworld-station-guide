## Why This Guide Exists

Outworld Station is an incredible game — but it doesn't hold your hand. The English-only UI, the unique Inventory Pairing system, the hidden mechanics — all of it adds up to a steep learning curve that the tutorial barely scratches.

We built this guide because **we needed it ourselves.** Every article on this site came from hours of trial and error, rebuilding half-finished production lines, and hunting through Steam discussions to find answers that should have been obvious.

## Who We Are

**Outworld Station Guide** is a small team of factory game veterans and Outworld Station enthusiasts. Between us:

- **500+ hours** of combined playtime across multiple saves (Station Level 15+)
- **Played every major factory game** — Factorio (800+ hrs), Satisfactory (300+ hrs), DSP (200+ hrs)
- **Tested every build** in version 1.0.0.2+ across fresh starts and late-game saves
- **Active in the community** — our guides incorporate tips from Reddit, Steam discussions, and direct testing

We're not affiliated with Trickjump Games. This is a fan-made resource, built by players for players.

## Our Editorial Standards

Every guide on this site follows the same process:

1. **Play it first** — No theorycrafting. We build the setup, run it for at least 2 hours, and document what actually works.
2. **Test the alternatives** — If there are multiple approaches (early-game vs late-game, cheap vs efficient), we cover both and explain the tradeoffs.
3. **Update with patches** — When the game updates, we verify that existing guides still work and update them if anything changed.
4. **Cite our sources** — Production rates, building costs, and game mechanics are verified in-game. External sources (dev news, community discoveries) are linked where relevant.
