## A Second Progress Bar For A Finished Station

By the time you finish the campaign, one thing is almost always true: your station works, and there is nothing left to build toward. **Station Prestige** is the answer v1.1.2.0 gives to that problem. It is a long-tail number that goes up when you do endgame work, and it pays out cosmetic and research rewards when it crosses a threshold.

If you have not unlocked the mission board yet, start with the [Arkship Terminal Missions Guide](/guides/arkship-terminal-missions-guide/). This page assumes missions are already available to you.

{{< callout type="verdict" title="The Short Version" >}}
**Station Prestige Points** are earned by completing Arkship Terminal missions. Points accumulate toward a **Station Prestige Level**. On reaching a new level you receive **random rewards**, confirmed to include new **Decoration Elements** and **Tech Points**. Treat it as an endless, optional endgame track: there is no fail state, no reset, and no downside to ignoring it if you would rather keep building.
{{< /callout >}}

## How Prestige Points Accrue

The single confirmed source of Prestige Points in v1.1.2.0 is **Arkship Terminal missions**. Complete a mission, collect the payout, repeat. There is no passive trickle, no conversion from credits, and no other currency you can trade in.

That single-source design is deliberate. It means the only way to raise Prestige is to engage with the new endgame content, which keeps the system from being brute-forced by a factory that already produces everything.

| Source | Gives Prestige? | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Arkship Terminal missions | Yes | The only confirmed source |
| Passive station production | No | Prestige does not accrue on its own |
| Credits / normal economy | No | Not convertible |
| Campaign milestones | No | Campaign unlocks the board; it does not pay Prestige |

## The Prestige Level Track

Prestige behaves like an experience bar. Points go in, the bar fills, and crossing a threshold raises your **Station Prestige Level**.

The important structural detail is the curve. New progression tracks almost always follow the same shape: **early levels come fast, later levels slow down**. That is good design for an endgame track because it gives an immediate sense of reward without capping out quickly. Do not expect a linear points-per-level cost -- expect the gaps between levels to widen.

{{< callout type="info" title="Beta Disclaimer" >}}
The exact point totals per level and the shape of the curve are still being tuned on the beta branch. The stable release may change both. This section describes the model, not confirmed numbers -- check back after the stable build for a full threshold table.
{{< /callout >}}

## What Each Level Grants

Level-up rewards are **random**, drawn from a confirmed pool:

| Reward category | Confirmed in 1.1.2.0? | What it does for you |
|-----------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Decoration Elements | Yes | New cosmetic pieces for your station |
| Tech Points | Yes | Research currency for the tech tree |
| Further random rewards | Yes (unspecified) | Additional rolled payouts |

The developer's earlier September 4 announcement also hinted at **resource nodes** and **station parts** as future reward categories. If those land, Prestige would eventually feed directly back into production rather than staying purely cosmetic and research-focused. That would make the track far more valuable than it looks today.

## Decoration Elements: The Real Endgame Prize

For a lot of players the headline is not Tech Points -- it is decorations. Before v1.1.2.0 there was essentially nothing to spend effort on once you had won. A finished station was a solved machine with no personal touch.

Decoration Elements change that. They give a completed base something to *become*. If you have ever wanted a showcase station -- a tidy residential ring, a furnished control deck, a monument to the campaign -- this is the currency that unlocks it.

Practical tip: claim decorations into a **dedicated plan area** rather than scattering them. Convert one section of your station into a showcase zone and let Prestige rewards accumulate there. It keeps the functional factory clean and gives the rewards a visible home.

## Tech Points: Feeding The Tree Backwards

Tech Points are the more utilitarian reward. If you finished the campaign with research still unfinished -- very common, since most players rush the campaign -- Prestige levels effectively let you backfill it.

This is worth calling out because it changes the optimal endgame order. Rather than grinding remaining research directly, you can let missions do the work and let Prestige levels top you up. Pair this with the [research order guide](/guides/research-order/) to decide which remaining nodes are actually worth the points.

## Should You Chase Prestige?

Yes, with one condition: only if you enjoy the mission loop itself.

The rewards are real but modest, and nothing about the main game is gated behind Prestige. Consider the tradeoffs directly:

| If you want... | Prestige is... |
|----------------|----------------|
| A reason to keep playing after the campaign | Worth it |
| Cosmetic self-expression for a finished base | Worth it |
| To finish leftover research passively | Worth it |
| Hard power or a new production tier | Not the right goal |
| Minimal effort / to stop playing | Skppable with no penalty |

There is genuinely no downside to ignoring the track. That is a feature, not a flaw -- it keeps the endgame optional, which is how it should be for a factory builder that already asks a lot of your time.

## A Levelling Routine

To move up the track efficiently:

1. **Read the terminal board every session.** Missions rotate, so check before you commit to anything else.
2. **Favour missions your automation can finish unattended.** The [endgame production patterns](/guides/endgame-final-stretch-guide/) you already built are the fastest way to clear objectives.
3. **Complete, collect, repeat.** Do not sit on completed missions; the payout compounds only when you level.
4. **Bank Tech Points for real nodes.** Spend on research that actually unlocks something, following the [tech-point strategy](/guides/tech-point-farming/).
5. **Route Decorations into a showcase zone.** Keep the reward visible and the factory clean.

## Bottom Line

Station Prestige is a second, optional progress bar for players who finished the story and wanted more. Points come only from Arkship missions, levels pay out random rewards -- most importantly Decoration Elements and Tech Points -- and the curve is tuned for a long, relaxed tail. Engage with it if the mission loop appeals to you; skip it guilt-free if it does not. Either way, your factory keeps running exactly as it did.
