## Your Gas Reserves Are Running Out Faster Than You Think

That starter gas vent near your station feeds your oxygen, nitrox, and chemical production. At Station Level 8, a single vent supports about 30 minutes of sustained chemical synthesis before depletion. The mid-game solution is not efficiency modules - it is a dedicated gas mining outpost three planets away.

Setting one up seems intimidating. It is not. Follow this checklist and you will have infinite gas in 45 minutes.

## Quick-Fix: The Gas Outpost Starter Pack

> **Ship these items to your gas outpost site:**
> - 4 Gas Extractors (one per gas type you need)
> - 2 Storage Tanks (buffer before shipping)
> - 1 Cargo Hub (drone logistics)
> - 1 Small Power Plant (solar + battery backup)
> - 5 Cargo Drones (dedicated to gas transport)
> 
> **Deployment time:** 45 minutes from first landing. **Gas output:** 150-200 units/hour per extractor.

## Setting Up the Outpost

### Step 1: Scout the Right Planet

Use the Star Map to find a gas giant or terran planet with at least 3 gas types. Prioritize planets within 2 wormhole jumps of your main station. Mark the coordinates.

### Step 2: Land and Deploy

Drop your starter pack items in this order:
1. Solar panels + battery (power first, always)
2. Gas Extractors on the highest-yield vents
3. Storage Tanks between extractors and the hub
4. Cargo Hub + assign drones

### Step 3: Configure Transport

Set up a dedicated freighter route between the outpost and your main station. Configure the route to run every 15 minutes or when storage tanks exceed 80% capacity.

### Step 4: Automate Supply

The outpost needs replacement parts and expansion materials. Set up a resupply drone that brings 50 of each common building part every station-level-up.

## FAQ

**Q: How many gas outposts do I need?**

One well-placed outpost with 3-4 extractors supports a mid-game station. Late-game needs 2 outposts: one for Oxygen/Nitrox, one for Helium-3/Hydrogen.

**Q: Do gas vents run out permanently?**

No. Gas vents regenerate over time. A depleted vent recovers to full capacity in about 2 hours of inactivity. Rotate between two vents for continuous supply.

**Q: Can I automate gas outpost defense?**

Yes. Place 2 missile turrets and 1 shield generator at each outpost. Link them to a dedicated ammo supply drone from the main station.
