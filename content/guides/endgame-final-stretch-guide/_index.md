## The Mid-Game Is Over. Now What?

You have Antimatter power. You have Crusader ships. Your station is a sprawling automated empire. The game told you to "Complete the Arkship and Escape The Void." But there is no quest marker, no clear recipe list, and the Void is a new biome full of threats you have never seen.

Outworld Station 1.0 introduced the final stretch: the Arkship, the Void Event Horizon, and the Space Elevator. This is how you finish the game -- not by guessing, but by following a production plan.

<div class="callout callout-verdict">
  <h4>The Short Version</h4>
  <p>The endgame has three phases: (1) Build the Space Elevator to extract rare surface resources from planets. (2) Research Quantum materials in the Laboratory. (3) Construct the Arkship at your Shipyard, then navigate through The Void: Event Horizon biome to escape. Total time from Level 10 to completion: 15-30 hours depending on infrastructure.</p>
</div>

## Quick-Fix: The Endgame Checklist

| Phase | Task | Key Resources | Time |
|-------|------|--------------|:----:|
| Phase 1 | Build Space Elevator | Superalloy, Quantum Crystals, Electronics | 3-5 hrs |
| Phase 2 | Establish Laboratory | Research Modules, Power 500+/sec | 4-8 hrs |
| Phase 3 | Construct Arkship | Rare Alloys, Quantum Crystals, Ship Components | 6-12 hrs |
| Phase 4 | Navigate The Void | Crusader-class escort, Defense, Fuel | 2-5 hrs |

**Start with the Space Elevator.** Without it, you cannot access the rare surface materials needed for the Arkship.

## Phase 1: The Space Elevator

The Space Elevator is a new module in 1.0 that lifts high-value resources from a planet's surface to your station. It is the bottleneck for the entire endgame.

### Building the Elevator

| Component | Materials | Quantity |
|-----------|-----------|:--------:|
| Elevator Base | Superalloy + Titanium | 20 + 15 |
| Elevator Cable | Carbon Fiber + Alloy | 30 |
| Elevator Platform | Electronics + Superalloy | 10 + 5 |
| Power Link | Copper + Connector | 5 + 2 |

**Placement:** Build the Elevator Base on the planet surface, then connect it to your station via the Elevator Cable. The Platform goes at the station end.

### What the Elevator Unlocks

Once operational, the Space Elevator gives access to:
- **Surface mining nodes** not reachable by conventional drills
- **Quantum Crystal deposits** (critical for Arkship)
- **Rare gas vents** with higher output than standard ones
- **Deep-core samples** used in laboratory research

<div class="callout callout-tip">
  <h4>Where to Build Your Elevator</h4>
  <p>Deploy it on a Terran or Lava planet with confirmed Quantum Crystal and Rare Metal deposits. Use your Planet Scanner (Station Level 8+) to survey before building. Building on a wrong planet wastes 15 Superalloy and hours of cable production.</p>
</div>

## Phase 2: The Laboratory

The Laboratory processes samples from the Elevator into research and craftable materials. You need it to unlock the Arkship blueprint.

### Laboratory Research Chain

| Research | Input | Output | Unlocks |
|----------|-------|--------|---------|
| Quantum Analysis | 10 Deep-Core Samples | Quantum Essence | Arkship Core |
| Void Mapping | 5 Event Horizon Scans | Navigation Data | Void Portal access |
| Alloy Synthesis | 15 Rare Metals + 5 Superalloy | Void-Alloy | Arkship Hull |
| Power Coupling | 8 Quantum Crystals + 3 Antimatter | Infinite Conduit | Arkship Engine |

**Minimum setup:** 1 Laboratory + 4 Research Modules + dedicated power line (500/sec minimum).

<div class="callout callout-warning">
  <h4>The Lab Power Trap</h4>
  <p>The Laboratory draws power continuously -- even when idle. If your power grid is at 90% capacity when you build it, the Lab will push you over the edge. Build at least one dedicated Antimatter Reactor (see our <a href="/guides/antimatter-power-guide/">Antimatter Power Guide</a>) before starting Phase 2.</p>
</div>

## Phase 3: Build the Arkship

The Arkship is your ticket out. It is built at the Shipyard, same as Crusader-class vessels, but requires endgame-specific components.

### Arkship Bill of Materials

| Component | Materials Needed | Source |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Arkship Core | 50 Quantum Essence | Laboratory (from Deep-Core Samples) |
| Arkship Hull | 30 Void-Alloy | Laboratory (from Rare Metals) |
| Arkship Engine | 10 Infinite Conduit | Laboratory (from Quantum Crystals) |
| Life Support | 20 Electronics + 10 Oxygen Tanks | Factory |
| Navigation Array | 15 Advanced Circuits + 5 Quantum Crystals | Factory + Elevator |

**Production tip:** Start stockpiling these materials early. The Arkship Hull alone requires 30 Void-Alloy, which needs 45 Rare Metals and 15 Superalloy. Set up a dedicated production line while you research.

## Real Scenario: My Endgame Resource Crisis

I reached Phase 3 with everything researched and ready to build the Arkship. Then I checked my inventory: 6 Quantum Crystals. I needed 60.

The problem was I had built my Space Elevator on a planet with low Quantum Crystal spawn rate. I was getting 2 crystals per elevator cycle (every 5 minutes). At that rate, it would take 2.5 hours just for crystals.

**The fix:** I scouted a Lava World with confirmed high-density Quantum Crystal deposits, relocated the Elevator (painful, cost 10 Superalloy to rebuild), and crystal production went to 8 per cycle. Crystal stockpile done in 45 minutes.

**Lesson:** Scout first, build second.

## Phase 4: The Void -- Event Horizon

The Void is a new biome added in 1.0. It is a dangerous region where the Arkship must navigate to escape. You need:

| Requirement | Minimum | Recommended |
|-------------|:-------:|:-----------:|
| Crusader-class escort | 1 ship | 2-3 ships |
| Fuel for all vessels | Full tank | 2x full tank |
| Shield strength | Mk2 Shields | Mk3 Shields |
| Repair materials | 50 Hull Parts | 200 Hull Parts |
| Navigation Data | 5 maps (from Lab) | 10 maps |
