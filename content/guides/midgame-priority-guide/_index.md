## You Have 20 Different Materials and No Idea Which Matter

By Station Level 6, you have unlocked Iron, Copper, Titanium, Tungsten, Electronics, Circuits, Superalloy, Chemicals, Nitrox, Hydrogen, and a dozen other materials flowing through your station. Every assembler needs something different. Every research requires a specific component. Every ship demands rare alloys.

The trap is treating all materials equally. Some materials are the backbone of your factory. Others are optional luxuries. If you stockpile the wrong items, your core production starves while your storage fills with things you barely use.

<div class="callout callout-verdict">
  <h4>The Short Version</h4>
  <p>Prioritize Iron and Copper (tier 1), then Electronics and Superalloy (tier 2), then circuits and advanced components (tier 3). Everything else is tier 4. Stockpile in that order. If tier 4 materials are competing with tier 1 for storage space, the tier 4 materials get dumped.</p>
</div>

### Tier 1: Foundation Materials

These run every production line. If they stop, everything stops.

| Material | Used By | Minimum Stockpile | Danger Zone |
|----------|---------|:-----------------:|:-----------:|
| Iron Plate | Everything | 2000 | < 500 |
| Copper Ingot | Electronics, Circuits | 1500 | < 400 |
| Iron Ore | Smelters | 3000 (mine buffer) | < 1000 |

**Priority:** These always get first access to connectors, buffer space, and drone routes.

### Tier 2: Core Components

These are your production multipliers. They unlock advanced buildings and ships.

| Material | Used By | Minimum Stockpile | Danger Zone |
|----------|---------|:-----------------:|:-----------:|
| Electronics | Assemblers, Ships, Research | 500 | < 150 |
| Superalloy | Ships, Advanced Buildings | 300 | < 100 |
| Circuit Array | Research, Ships | 200 | < 50 |

**Priority:** Dedicated production lines. If Electronics and Decorations compete for Titanium, Electronics wins.

### Tier 3: Advanced Materials

Needed for late-game tech but not essential for daily operations.

| Material | Used By | Minimum Stockpile | Danger Zone |
|----------|---------|:-----------------:|:-----------:|
| Titanium | Superalloy, Ship armor | 500 | < 200 |
| Tungsten | Superalloy, Advanced tools | 300 | < 100 |
| Chemicals | Processing, Ammo | 400 | < 150 |

**Priority:** Produce on-demand with small buffers. Do not let them fill your main storage.

### Tier 4: Luxury and Niche

| Material | Used By | Stockpile Strategy |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| Ice | Water, Coolant | Small buffer, 100-200 |
| Carbon | Filters, Life support | Small buffer, 100-200 |
| Glass | Decorations | Skip unless specifically needed |
| Biological Samples | Special research | Keep 50, use on demand |

### Why Most Players Stall at Level 8

Station Level 8 is where your resource consumption outpaces your production. You unlocked new blueprints, added more assemblers, and started building ships. Your drills cannot keep up. Your smelters cannot keep up. Your storage is full of low-tier materials you thought you needed, but the high-tier stuff keeps draining.

### How to Cross the Gap

1. **Double your drills** before adding new assemblers. Most players add assemblers first. Wrong order. Drills first.
2. **Purge tier 4 materials** from main storage. Move them to satellite storage or deconstruct them.
3. **Build dedicated production lines.** Do NOT share iron between smelter 1 and smelter 2. Dedicated connectors for each.
4. **Check ratios.** One drill feeds roughly 2 smelters. One smelter feeds roughly 1.5 assemblers (iron) or 1 assembler (copper).

### Gap Signs Checklist

- [ ] Assemblers idle more than 30% of the time
- [ ] Buffer tanks below 30% for core materials
- [ ] Drone network reporting backlogged routes
- [ ] You are manually moving materials between buildings
- [ ] Research lab waiting for components

## Priority When Expanding

Every time you build something new, ask: "Is this tier 1-2 or tier 3-4?"

| New Building | Tier | Build Priority | Notes |
|-------------|:----:|:--------------:|-------|
| Iron Drill | 1 | Highest | More iron fixes most problems |
| Copper Smelter | 1 | Highest | Follow iron expansion |
| Electronics Assembler | 2 | High | Enables most upgrades |
| Superalloy Forge | 2 | High | Unlocks ship and advanced buildings |
| Chemical Plant | 3 | Medium | Needed for ammo and processing |
| Shipyard | 3 | Medium | Build after components are stable |
| Decoration Fab | 4 | Lowest | Station looks matter later |
| Extra Storage | 2 | High | If any buffer is below 30% |

<div class="callout callout-synergy">
  <h4>The 80% Rule</h4>
  <p>When any tier 1 material buffer drops below 80%, stop building tier 3-4 items until it recovers. You do not need better-looking walls while your smelters are starved for iron ore.</p>
</div>

## Station Level Transition Points

| Station Level | Priority Shift | What Changes |
|:-------------:|---------------|--------------|
| 1-5 | Build everything | You need a bit of everything. Keep 200 of each. |
| 6-7 | Focus iron + copper | Production lines stabilize. Triple iron production. |
| 8-9 | Add electronics + superalloy | Mid-game components become critical. |
| 10-12 | Balance all tiers | By now you should have dedicated lines for each tier. |
| 13+ | Optimize | Micro-manage ratios. Purge inefficiencies. |
