Outworld Station Combat Ship Loadout - Best Weapons and Modules for Space Fighting

Outworld Station combat ship weapons, shield modules, and thruster loadouts that actually work against drone raids and PvE enemies. Tested through Station Level 12.

Combat Ships

Your Ship Has Guns. That Does Not Mean It Can Fight.

You finished building your first combat ship. Slapped on the biggest gun you could craft, filled the cargo with ammo, and launched to clear that raid swarm. Then you watched your ship get shredded in 30 seconds while barely scratching the enemy shields.

Combat in Outworld Station is not about biggest gun wins. It is about matching weapon type to enemy type, balancing shield regen against fire rate, and ensuring your thruster output lets you actually hit moving targets. I lost three ships learning this.

The Short Version

Use Laser Cannons against shielded drones, Missile Launchers against slow heavy targets, and always equip at least one Thruster Upgrade. A 2x Laser + 1x Shield + 1x Thruster setup handles 90% of fights through Station Level 10.

The Thruster Secret

Most players put a second weapon instead of a thruster upgrade. This is a mistake. A ship that cannot track fast drones is a ship that wastes ammo. One thruster upgrade increases your effective DPS by ~40% because you actually hit what you aim at.

Module Placement and Ship Building Order‚ñ?/span>

Build Order for Your First Combat Ship

  1. Hull - Start with a Small Combat Hull (fastest, cheapest)
  2. Shield Generator - Install BEFORE weapons. An unshielded ship is a corpse.
  3. Thruster Upgrade - Determines your turn rate
  4. Primary Weapon - Laser Cannon (no ammo to manage)
  5. Secondary Weapon - Add only after primary + shield + thruster are installed
  6. Ammo Storage - If using missile or bullet weapons, add extra cargo

Common Build Mistakes

The "All Guns" Trap

Every new player puts 4 weapons on a ship with no shield. The ship dies before firing the second salvo. One shield + one thruster is worth more than any second weapon.

What to Skip

  • Extra Cargo - Combat ships die too fast to benefit from extra storage
  • Mining Lasers - Different weapon category, useless in ship-to-ship combat
  • Multiple Shield Types - Shield generators do not stack. One big shield beats two small.

Ammo Management: The Hidden Tax

Missile Ammo and Bullets are consumable. If your combat ship goes through ammo faster than you produce it, your ship becomes a paperweight.

Ammo Independence Tip

Build a Laser Cannon as your primary weapon until Station Level 10. The power draw is higher, but you eliminate the entire ammo logistics chain. Your ship can fight indefinitely as long as your station has power.

When to Fight vs When to Run

Not every raid needs a combat ship response. Early game (Station Level 4-6), your station turrets + drone shields handle most threats. Mid game (Level 7-9), you need a ship for the big waves. Late game (Level 10+), automated point defense does most of the work and your ship is for cleanup only.

Pair with Station Defense

Your combat ship works best as a rapid response unit, not a permanent patrol. Keep it docked and fueled. When a raid warning appears, launch and engage the heaviest targets first while your turrets handle the swarm.
